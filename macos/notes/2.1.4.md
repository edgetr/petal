Petal AI can read what's on a page, notes stay out of each other's way, and after an update Petal shows you what changed.

## Petal AI
- It reads the words on the page, document or thread you're on. Ask a note "@Petal summarize this", or ask about a video and it reads the page and its transcript.
- A note on a browser tab finds its tab while the tab plays sound. Before, it said the tab wasn't showing.
- When Petal can't see something yet, it asks macOS for the permission itself instead of sending you to Settings.
- A question in a note on a GitHub page is answered as a question. It becomes a coding job only when you ask for work.
- "Remind me at 5", "tonight at 9", "on the 20th" and "next month" work, and a reminder is never set for a time that's already gone. With no time, Petal asks when.
- The AI button shows when the quick question is still working, and when its answer is waiting.

## Notes
- Papers beside a window never cover one another. A new note takes free room, and one you moved stays where you left it.
- An answer sits right under the line that asked.
- Put a note away from its menu, or press Escape on a folded one. Its tab brings it back.
- A new, empty note stays while you click elsewhere.
- A window is made narrower for a note once, not every time the note opens and folds.
- An empty note says what it's for, and the @ in its corner starts a line for Petal AI.
- The Petal Notes window has "+ Note".

## The dock
- Magnification works.
- An app that's running stays in the dock when it's hidden or has no window.
- Drag an app off the dock to remove it. Drop an app, a folder or a web address on it to add it.
- An app's menu has Open, the app's own "New…" item, and Force Quit while you hold Option.

## Settings and updates
- After an update, What's New opens once. It's always in the menu bar's menu and in Settings › About.
- With updates off, Check now tells you what's available and installs only when you say so.
- New switches: Finder's button, and the "Ask Petal AI" button by selected text.
- Deleting a saved login or a routine asks first. Importing macOS's Dock adds to yours instead of replacing it.
- Turning off "Let other AI agents change Petal" takes effect at once.
- The AI button shows whenever you've chosen an AI.
- Searching Settings finds more of what you type, and Return opens it.

## Also
- Clicking an app's icon again goes through every one of its windows.
- An icon bounces only when its badge really goes up.
- Petal AI asks before it saves or runs a routine that opens things.
- A login saved on a page of this Mac keeps its port, and the vault's secrets stay out of menu names and window titles Petal AI reads.

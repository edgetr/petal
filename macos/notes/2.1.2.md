A new Settings window and a new welcome, and Petal no longer quits when it's checked.

## Settings, rebuilt
- One window of glass, not cards on glass. Each part of Petal has its own page and color: Dock, Look, Notes, Petal AI, Vault, Routines, Privacy, About.
- The bar across the top searches every setting as you type, and Return still asks Petal AI.
- Look shows your dock right there, changing as you drag size, magnification and color.
- Notes has a page of its own; you pick paper or Liquid Glass by clicking a paper.
- Privacy gathers the apps kept private, what Petal AI did, what it remembers, and what macOS lets Petal see.
- About half the words, and every switch in one line down the right.

## A welcome that asks for less
- The first launch shows Petal's four parts and asks only what it must: your Dock, your AI, your windows.
- Tabs and now playing are asked for when you first use them. Now playing appears once you allow it from Music's or Spotify's preview in the dock.

## Fixed
- **Check Petal** in Settings › About, and `petal doctor`, could make the dock quit a moment later. It has done so since Petal's first release. Fixed.
- The pale glow behind each settings page's icon is gone.

## Also
- Your AI is called Petal AI throughout; Claude and Codex are named where you choose one.
- One set of text sizes and corners across the app. Your notes look the same.
- An empty chat shows three things to ask and nothing else.

Petal needs macOS 26 or later. Downloaded in a browser, it needs System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway once.

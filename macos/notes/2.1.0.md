Petal AI now works out of your way: each run gets a hidden display of its own.

## A display of its own for each run
- When a helper in a note, or one of your own AI sessions, opens an app or a window, it opens on a hidden display made for that run. You don't see it, and your pointer can't wander onto it.
- Point at the AI button to watch: each run shows its display live, with Stop and Show me, which brings its window to your screen while it carries on.
- Questions, Buy cards and the vault's card still come to you, over the dock or on the note.
- When the run ends its display goes. An app it started that you never used is quit; anything else comes back to your screen, never lost.
- Several runs can work at once without seeing each other. Two that want the same app take turns, and your own use of an app always comes first.
- How many run at once depends on your Mac's memory. Past that, the next one waits its turn.
- It's on by default. Settings › AI turns it off.

## A browser of its own
- A run that needs the web gets its own copy of a browser you have installed (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi or Helium), with a fresh profile, on its display. Petal works in it without your pointer or keyboard, and nothing comes to the front.
- The vault works there under the same rules as everywhere: a login only on its own site, a card only after you say Buy, and Petal AI never sees a password or a card number.
- It closes when the run ends and leaves nothing behind.

## Also
- Petal AI's pointer can be any color: ask it to "make your cursor blue", or pick one in Settings.
- Asking for an update that's already downloaded no longer downloads it twice.

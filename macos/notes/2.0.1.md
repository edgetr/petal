Petal 2 brings Petal Notes, and makes the dock simpler: one dock, and no modes.

## Petal Notes
- Your own writing on paper, stuck where it matters: a sentence, a browser tab, a window, an app, a file, a person you're chatting with, or the screen itself.
- Drag a paper off the note pad in the dock and let it go anywhere, press ⌃⌥N, or choose Note by selected text. Pick a note up from anywhere on it and it stays where you leave it.
- Each note is a Markdown file and each notebook a folder, in your iCloud Drive. The Petal Notes window, from the mark in the menu bar, holds them all: search, a grid, or a canvas you lay out freely.
- A notebook can be Private, and can sit in the dock as a stack.

## Helpers in notes
- Write @Petal and what you want on a line, then press Return. It works in the background, and its answer comes back as ink on the note.
- A note takes its instructions in plain words: "bring this back Friday", "check this every morning", "put this away".
- On a pull request or a file in a git repository, it runs your own Claude Code or Codex in a worktree of its own.

## Petal's viewer
- Open a PDF, a Word document, a deck or a spreadsheet in a window of Petal's, with your notes in the margin beside the passage they're about.
- A note there can follow what you select and explain it as you read.

## One dock, no modes
- Modes are gone from Petal for macOS, and with them focus cards, held notifications, drafted replies, the schedule and the day's summary. Petal no longer needs Full Disk Access.
- Your default mode's apps are now your dock, the same on every Desktop. The other modes' app lists are not kept.
- The quick question always opens over the dock. To keep an answer somewhere, choose Stick here and it becomes a note.

## Fixes
- Quitting Petal, or restarting it for an update, no longer hangs.
- In Mission Control, Petal's dock steps aside and comes back when you leave.

Safer limits for Petal AI and other AI agents, and macOS's Dock comes back when you choose it.

## Safety
- What only you decide stays yours. Full access, the apps Petal AI may use and the apps it must never look at are kept in your Keychain, so another program changing Petal's settings file can't give an AI more than you did. An AI agent can still ask for less.
- Agents' access to your vault is kept in your Keychain too. Each agent asks once more the first time it needs a key after this update.
- Petal runs Claude Code or Codex from a path you chose only when that program is signed by its maker. Settings › Petal AI says when a path was refused.
- Open in Browser on a page Petal AI made opens a safe copy that runs no scripts.
- Petal AI can't read, view or comment on a Private notebook, from any of its tools.
- A routine that opens something, added by editing its file, stays off until you say yes on its card.
- Petal AI opens only an app by name, a web address, or a document or folder by its full path. Pages it makes only show; they run nothing and fetch nothing.
- An API key fills by itself only in the app you typed it for. Any other app asks first.

## The dock
- Choosing macOS's Dock brings it back even if Petal's hiding had been left on it, and `petal restore-dock` fixes that too.

## Notes
- A notebook's cover can't be moved out of its notebook, and only the real cover note counts as one.
- Two quick changes to the same note no longer leave a conflict copy behind.

# Petal.app: the vault, agents outside Petal and secrets in pictures

In `macos/Sources/Petal/` (the app's overview is in `app-core.md`):

- The vault (`petal_vault`, Settings › Vault): logins, contact info (with a relationship, "girlfriend", and one contact that is the person, "me"), addresses (each may belong to a contact; a contact can have several), payment info and API keys, all in the Keychain under `tech.edgetr.petal.vault` (one item each, account `item:<id>`, the item as JSON, marked `petal.vault.item.1` in its generic attribute), never in a plain file; every Keychain read and write on `VaultStore.queue`, one at a time, never the main thread, and a read a save overtook is never cached. A read the Keychain refuses, whole or in part, is an error (`VaultStore.Unreadable`), never a shorter vault: it isn't cached, export and import refuse, Settings says "Your vault can't be read right now", and everything the AI would be shown says so instead (`SecretIndex.unavailable`: nothing counts as checked; `petal_ui` and pictures refuse). Older name→secret items (any name, even one starting `item:`), and items of a kind this Petal doesn't know, are unsorted (`VaultKind.unsorted`) on the first load; nothing that doesn't read as an item is dropped. Petal can't tell what an unsorted secret is or where it belongs: the AI sees only its title and "unsorted", filling it asks every time, anywhere (even with Full access), and it still works by its old name through `use`; Settings › Vault lists them under All ("Tell Petal what each one is", What is it?: a login, an API key or a card number, the secret moved there, the same id), and once sorted the normal rules apply. `Vault.swift` is its pure side (what the AI sees, sites and registrable names, the fill decisions, importers and exporters, password making), checked by `macos/checks/run.sh`; `VaultStore.swift` the Keychain and the settings window's model; `VaultFill.swift` the tool and the filling; `VaultViews.swift` the card and the settings page. The AI sees kinds, titles, sites, usernames, contact info and addresses whole, and a card's name, last four and expiry; never a password, card number, security code, key or notes, and all of those stay in UIMap.SecretIndex's redaction (of notes, what reads as a code: 8 characters or more going from letters to digits and back at least three times, found whole as a word of its own, `Vault.noteCodes`; never a contact's or an address's notes, a part of an email address or host name, or the item's own username; and numbers of 6 to 19 digits, whole or in groups, in a line or sentence of the notes that names codes and no phone, `Vault.noteNumbers`, searched for as secrets are; the person's other words in a note are told elsewhere as they are). Petal fills a field of the window `petal_ui` looked at (through ComputerUse's line: pointer, Stop, the window's fingerprint, the page's address read again right before the keys go; keystrokes, never the clipboard; a country picked from a menu), and decides itself from what it reads (the nearest web area's `AXURL`, the page's, the browser's tab address, believed only in a browser `VaultReader.browsers` lists whose signature has its maker's Team ID; any other app's web view is that app; an app's bundle id and the Team ID that signed it, saved with the login, `appSigners`), never from the AI: a page that a browser Petal drives itself reports as kept apart from its site (a sandboxed document or frame: `Vault.Place.isolated`) is never that site, whatever its address: a login or key there asks, payment info never fills, nothing is saved for the site; **a login fills without asking only on its own site** (the same host or under its registrable name by the Public Suffix List, `macos/Resources/public_suffix_list.dat`, ICANN and private sections, copied into the app by build.sh; until it's read, or for a name it doesn't cover, only the saved host and under it; https, or http on this Mac: localhost, 127.0.0.1, [::1]; a login saved on a page of this Mac keeps its port, "localhost:3000", and fills by itself on that port only, since each port is another program's page; one the person wrote without a port, typed or in an imported file, is every port's) **or its own app, signed by the same Team ID**, an API key on its service's site, this Mac's http(s) pages or the app it was typed for on Petal's card (that app by the same Team ID; any other app asks: an app keeps or sends on what's typed into it); a site is a web address or a bare host, never another kind of address (an export's `android://` entry names none), and a card shows a name with letters outside ASCII with the form it's sent as beside it (`VaultSite.shown`: "аррӏе.com (xn--80ak6aa92e.com)"); anywhere else one card asks for all of a fill's fields, the mismatch said plainly, Don't the default; one fill's fields must be in one page or app. **Payment info** goes only into https pages, and only after one "Buy on <host>?" card per checkout (what Petal AI says it's buying, in its own words, marked as such, and the card; Don't the default, no Always): Buy covers that card on that exact host and page (path), in that window and tab (the browser's tab id where it gives one, and the tab's outermost web area), for the rest of the reply (`Vault.Checkout`; a new Buy card for that window ends the Buy already in it, whatever the answer; the window seen on any other page or tab ends it, checked by every read of it (`petal_look`, `petal_ui`) before it answers, and before every fill and every act there, and coming back asks again; Stop and Escape end it, and keep standing while it lasts) and doesn't let the AI use the browser beyond what its own Allow does; before Buy nothing of the card is typed. Contact info and addresses fill freely. What's missing is asked for on a card the person types into (`request`; "Save to your vault" on by default when it fills the page; the site shown and saved is the one Petal read; the card says when that page or app doesn't match); `new_login` makes a strong password (SecRandomCopyBytes), saves it, then fills it, only where every field would fill by itself. Every step of a vault call checks it's still in the reply it came from (`ComputerUse.token`). Every secret Petal types is hidden from the AI for as long as Petal runs, saved or not (`VaultIndex.remember`). The older `use` goes through `fill`. Showing a saved secret in Settings asks for Touch ID or the Mac's password (LocalAuthentication); so does an export (Petal's JSON with everything, or logins as Chrome's CSV, which Apple Passwords imports too). No 2FA codes yet: the AI reads a code from mail with `petal_ui`.
- Agents outside Petal and the vault (`AgentAccess.swift`, its pure side, checked by `macos/checks/run.sh`; `AgentCallers.swift`; `AgentVault.swift`; `AgentRun.swift`): Claude Code, Codex or opencode in T3 Code or a terminal use `petal_vault` (through `Petal --mcp`) exactly as Petal AI does, and `petal run --key "<title or id>" [--env NAME] [--stdin] -- <command…>` runs a command with API keys. **Who's asking is Petal's to work out, never the caller's to say:** the socket's audit token (`LOCAL_PEERTOKEN`) gives the process that connected, which must be Petal's own code (its designated requirement, checked now) to be handed a key; its folder (`proc_pidinfo`) is the project (the nearest git repository, a worktree's main checkout only when git made it one: its `.git` file's gitdir, links resolved, is an entry in that repository's `.git/worktrees/` whose own `gitdir` points back at the `.git` in this very folder; a `.git` that is a symbolic link is never followed; else the folder); the processes above it give the agent (every one's signature read, but macOS's own programs in /bin, /sbin, /usr/bin, /usr/sbin, /usr/libexec and /System; agents under /usr/local and /opt are checked like any other; Claude Code only when signed by Anthropic, Team ID Q6L2SF6YDW, identifier `com.anthropic.claude-code`; Codex by OpenAI, 2DC432GLL2, `codex`; opencode by its name, said so on the card; a program named like one but signed otherwise is "an unknown program"; no agent above it, "a command") and the app it runs in (the outermost app bundle: T3 Code, Terminal, Ghostty…). **Petal AI is only the tools of an AI Petal itself started:** Petal records each AI process it starts for a chat or a helper (its pid, when it started and the chat it's for: `AgentCallers.started`, from `Agent.run`), and a caller is that chat (`Caller.note`) only when it asks for one of Petal AI's tools (`assist`) and the process right above it is that very process, Claude Code or Codex signed by its maker, with Petal right above (or, where what Petal started is the agent's launcher, as npm's `codex` script is, the signed agent that launcher started): `AgentAccess.ownChat`. Petal being somewhere above is never enough, and what a caller says its chat is counts for nothing. A shell, a hook, another tool server or a command a chat runs is the agent above it ("Claude Code in Petal"), asked like that agent anywhere else, and `petal run` is never Petal AI, whoever runs it. Any other caller is `mcp-<pid>` of what connected. Who it is goes with each request from the socket to every card and gate (never looked up again later); a request without it is refused. Petal's own chats keep the vault's rules. Any other caller's first use of an item in a project (a fill, `new_login` as "new logins", or `petal run`'s keys, one card for all) asks: "Codex in T3 Code wants your Anthropic key for “npm test” in ~/petal" (a command too long for that line is said whole under it, up to 200 characters, and a character that wouldn't show as itself, such as one that turns the reading direction around, is written as its number: `AgentAccess.commandLine`, `keyCommand`), with Always for this project (only for a verified agent, Claude Code or Codex signed by its maker, and never the home folder and above; opencode, a command and an unknown program are allowed once at a time), Allow once, and Don't the default; then every fill rule applies unchanged. Grants (item id, project, a verified agent or any agent, which covers only verified agents; no secret) are kept in `Application Support/Petal/agent-grants.json` (`PETAL_AGENT_GRANTS` for testing), listed in Settings › Vault › Agents with Remove, and can be added there ahead of time; every use goes into What Petal AI did. Each use carries what allowed it (`AgentAccess.Authorization`, held in an `AgentPass`: a grant's id, or that card's Allow once, good for that one operation only and spent when it's done), checked again right before each field is filled and before keys go (`AgentAccess.stillAllowed`: the grant still there and covering the item, project and agent); removing a grant in Settings revokes every operation relying on it, even one waiting on another card or filling (as a Stop would). `petal run` gets the keys over the socket (`["agentKeys", json]`; read again right before they go, so an item edited, replaced or deleted, or a grant removed, while the card waited gives nothing), refuses a key under 16 characters (`Redactor.minimumKey`: too short to find in every form; it's filled into a field instead), puts each in its service's variable (`AgentAccess.variable`: ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, OPENAI_API_KEY, GEMINI_API_KEY, XAI_API_KEY, GITHUB_TOKEN, STRIPE_SECRET_KEY; else `--env` is needed; two keys that would go in one variable are refused before anything is asked) or on the command's input, never prints one, passes the exit code (or 128 + the signal) and signals on, and streams the command's output through `AgentAccess.Redactor`: each key as itself, hex, percent-encoded (each character as itself or %XX in either case, or a space as "+": whatever Python's `quote`/`quote_plus` or JavaScript's `encodeURI`/`encodeURIComponent` keep) and base64 (standard and URL-safe, at each of the three alignments, and wrapped onto lines, as MIME, PEM and an indented YAML block write it: up to 8 blanks, then maybe a line break and an indent of up to 64, between its characters; the whole span) becomes `[<title> key]`, with the end of a read that could start a key held back for the next. It keeps keys out of the agent's transcript and logs, so the person needn't rotate them; a command written to leak one still can. Showing a key's text to an agent stays refused. `PETAL_SOCKET` points a client (`petal run`, `Petal --mcp`) at another socket, for testing.
- `Sensitive.swift`: `petal_look`'s pictures without secrets: password fields, text fields named for a password, token, key, card number or security code (never a window or page for its name), any text holding a vault secret, and every field Petal filled with one are blacked out in memory (found through Accessibility right before and after the picture, matched element by element: one that moved is covered all the way between; one that appeared, went or moved over a quarter of the window means no picture, or on the screen that window covered) before the PNG is written; a window that can't be read whole in time (children Accessibility didn't give, deeper than the scan goes, the vault unreadable) is no picture (on the screen, it's left out), and so are menus, the menu bar and other overlays with no Accessibility window of their own. The redaction index (`VaultIndex`) is made again while the vault or what Petal typed changes under it, and redacted text is checked against the generation it was made with (`UIMap.settled`). A card number is found across any separators between its digits and in any script's digits.

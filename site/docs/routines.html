Routines

Tell Petal a rule once in plain words and it keeps running in the background — sorting files, tidying folders, opening or hiding apps and running Shortcuts.

Making a routine

Open Settings › Routines › “Make a routine” and choose “Ask Petal AI”. Describe what you want in plain words, such as:

  • “Keep my Downloads tidy”
  • “When a PDF lands in Downloads, file it under Receipts”
  • “When I open Xcode, hide Messages”

Petal AI turns your words into a routine once, when saving. After that, runs use no AI at all — except when sorting files by what they are.

Triggers and actions

A routine can start when:

  • A file lands in a folder, matched by type or name.
  • An app opens or comes to the front.
  • A set time arrives, on the days you choose.

When triggered, a routine can:

  • Sort files by kind, or by what they are.
  • Move files to a folder.
  • Open apps, web addresses or documents (never a script or program).
  • Hide apps.
  • Run a Shortcut.

Routines act on new files as they arrive; they touch existing files only when you ask.

Safety and undoing moves

Petal asks before saving a routine that opens something or runs a Shortcut, and asks before tidying 10 or more files at once.

Files are only moved — never deleted, and never moved over another file. Everything a routine did appears in Settings › Routines › “What they did”, with “Put back” to undo any move.

Managing routines

In Settings › Routines:

  • Switch “Run my routines” off to pause every routine at once.
  • Under “Your routines”, each entry shows its words, “Last ran …”, an on/off switch and × to delete it.

Deleting a routine leaves any moved files where they are, and you can still put them back from “What they did”.

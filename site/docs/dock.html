The dock

Petal gives you one dock with the same apps on every Desktop and one on each screen. When you add many apps, icons shrink to fit.

What sits in the dock

From one end to the other, the dock holds:

  • The AI button (when an AI is chosen), with its usage ring; press ⌃⌥Space to open Petal AI.
  • Finder.
  • The note pad (while Petal Notes is on); click for a new note, like ⌃⌥N, or drag a paper off it to place one.
  • Your kept apps, followed by open apps you didn't keep.
  • + to add apps, folders or websites.
  • Now playing for Music and Spotify, with album art.
  • Minimized windows as thumbnails.
  • Folder stacks, with Downloads and Screenshots by default.
  • Trash.

Adding, opening and removing

Click + to search (“Add an app, folder or website…”), or drag an app, folder or web address straight onto the dock. A folder becomes a stack showing its newest files first; with “Download progress on stacks” on, active downloads show a progress ring. Drop documents onto an app to open them in it, and drag an app off the dock to remove it.

Website shortcuts fetch the site's own icon, show a small globe and open in your default browser. Right-click one for “Open in Browser” or “Remove from Dock”.

Previews, badges and menus

Hover over a running app to see live window previews (requires Screen Recording). Badges appear on app icons, and with “Bounce” on an icon bounces once when its badge goes up.

  • Right-click an app for its “Windows” (when “Its windows” is on), “Recent” files, “Open”, its own new-item command (such as “New Message”), “Keep in Dock” or “Remove from Dock”, “Show in Finder”, “Minimize All”, “Hide” and “Quit” (hold Option for “Force Quit”).
  • Right-click a minimized window for “Show” or “Close Window”.
  • Right-click now playing for “Previous”, “Next” or “Show Player”.
  • Right-click the dock's background for “Position” (Left, Bottom or Right), “Auto-hide”, “Anything else? Ask Petal AI…”, “Petal Settings…”, “Use macOS’s Dock” and “Quit Petal”.

Full screen and Mission Control

On a full-screen Space, Petal's dock steps out of the way and comes out when you reach the screen's edge. In Mission Control, macOS's own Dock appears.

Caveat. In full screen, macOS's own Dock can still slide out at the screen's edge. macOS does not let Petal turn that off.

Tiling windows

Turn on Settings › Dock › “Tile windows” (off by default) to make windows sit side by side and fill the screen. Turning it off leaves every window where it is. Dialogs, sheets, fixed-size, minimized and full-screen windows stay floating.

Drag a window onto another to swap them, or use the keyboard:

  • ⌃⌥ with an arrow key moves focus to the window in that direction.
  • ⌃⌥⇧ with an arrow key swaps places with that window.
  • ⌃⌥T floats or tiles the front window.
  • ⌃⌥R evens out the layout.

Switching back to macOS's Dock

Choose “Use macOS’s Dock” from the menu-bar mark or the dock's background menu, or change “Which dock” in Settings › Dock.

In Terminal. Run petal dock macos to switch to macOS's Dock, or petal dock petal to switch back.

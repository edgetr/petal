Petal Notes

Petal Notes stick to whatever you are working on, stay as plain Markdown files in iCloud Drive, and work with or without AI.

Making a note

  • Click the note pad in the dock (“New note · drag it anywhere”) for a note on what's in front, ready to write in.
  • Drag a paper off the note pad and drop it onto a window or a Desktop. Hold Option while dropping to keep it on the screen itself (“Stays on screen”).
  • Press ⌃⌥N anywhere (while “Keyboard shortcuts” is on) to note the selected sentence, or else the front tab, file, window or Desktop.
  • Select text in any app and choose “Note” from the small bar that appears (never shown in private apps or password fields).
  • Drag a paper out of the AI button, or choose “Stick here” after an answer in the AI panel.

When one of Petal's own windows is in front, a new note goes on the Desktop.

Where notes stick

A note can stick to a sentence, a browser tab, a window, all of an app (“all of <App>”), a file in any app, a Desktop, the screen itself (“On screen”, keeping its spot on every Desktop on this Mac), or a person (“<name>, in any chat app”, shown beside their conversation in Messages, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack or Mail). One note can stick to several places.

On a window, notes rest as tabs along its right edge. Click a tab to open it beside the window; click away to fold it to a small square. Choose “Put away” in the letterhead menu, or press Esc on a folded paper, to hide it until its tab or the Petal Notes window brings it back. Drag a paper by any spot that isn't a control or text; it stays where you let go. Notes untouched for 30 days fade to a line.

Writing and menus

Notes hold headings, checkboxes, links that turn into cards, and pasted or dropped pictures. An empty note shows “Write, or type @ to ask Petal AI” (see Helpers in notes). If you leave a note empty, it moves to the Trash when you put it away or leave its window — never on a stray click elsewhere.

The letterhead's place menu offers “Stuck to”, “Open in Petal's viewer”, “Open this page”, “Take it off here”, “Move it to”, “Also stick it to”, “Move it to this Desktop”, “Keep it on screen”, “Put away” and “Move to Trash”. Its notebook menu moves the note, creates a “New notebook…” or sets “<Notebook>'s color” (your Mac's accent color, Blue, Purple, Pink, Orange, Yellow, Green or Graphite).

Drop a note onto an app in the dock to use it: Mail starts a draft, Calendar asks “Add “<title>” to Calendar?” (“Add” / “Don't”), and any other app puts the note's words on your clipboard. Nothing is sent on its own.

Notebooks and the Petal Notes window

Open the Petal Notes window from the menu-bar mark's “Petal Notes” or a notebook's stack. Search with “Search every note”, check the “Coming back”, “Watching” and “Helpers at work” shelves, and view each notebook's cover and its notes as a grid or a canvas (“Add an area”, “Write on the canvas”, with areas, arrows and words).

“Inbox” is the default notebook and can't be renamed or trashed. A notebook's menu has “Rename…”, “Color”, “Show as a grid” / “Show as a canvas”, “Put new notes here”, “Private”, “In the dock as a stack” (a pile of paper in its color whose newest papers fan up), “Show in Finder” and “Move to Trash…”. Switch between paper and glass in Settings › Notes › “Paper or Liquid Glass”.

Private notebooks. A “Private” notebook is kept from Petal AI and note tools (unless asked from inside that notebook), from previews and from Petal's own pictures. It does not hide notes from other apps' screen recordings.

Each note is a Markdown file and each notebook is a folder in iCloud Drive's “Petal Notes” folder (changeable in Settings › Notes › “Kept in”). If iCloud creates two versions, both appear with “Keep this one”.

Petal's viewer

Choose “Open in Petal's viewer” from a note's place menu to read documents with margin notes: PDFs with exact passages (select text, then “Note”), and Word, PowerPoint, Keynote, Excel and other formats through Quick Look with notes per page or slide. Use “Open in <app>” for the original app. The viewer reloads when a file changes (“Updated just now”) and never moves or alters it.

In a margin note's menu, “Follow what I select” sends each selection to Petal AI with what the note says to do (or explains it plainly when the note is empty), showing a short answer beside the paragraph.

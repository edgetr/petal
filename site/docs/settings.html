Settings

Open “Petal Settings…” from the menu-bar mark or by right-clicking the dock. Use “Ask Petal AI, or search settings” at the top to find any row or ask Petal AI to change it.

Dock

Choose “Which dock” to use, then configure The dock across seven groups:

  • “Where it sits”: “Position”, “Auto-hide”, “Keep windows clear of the dock” (when available), “Open Petal at login” and “Petal in the menu bar”.
  • “Apps”: “Show open apps from this Desktop only”, “Window previews”, “Show minimized windows”, “Badges”, “Bounce” and “Drop files on an app to open them”.
  • “Right-click menu”: “Its windows” and “Recent files”.
  • “Extras”: “Now playing”, “Folder stacks”, “Add a folder” and “Download progress on stacks” (when stacks are on), “Finder” and “Trash”.
  • “Windows”: “Tile windows”, plus “Gaps” and “Tiling keys” when tiling is on.
  • “Shortcuts”: “Keyboard shortcuts”, plus “Shortcut keys” when shortcuts are on.
  • “Bring your Dock along”: “Import macOS’s Dock” to bring in any missing apps or stacks.

Look

A live dock preview sits above three groups:

  • “Icons”: “Size”, “Dim apps that aren’t open”, “Glow” and “Dividers”.
  • “Under the pointer”: “Magnification”, “How much”, “Lift on hover” and “Names on hover”.
  • “Color”: “Dock tint”, “Accent color” and “Petal AI’s pointer”.

Notes

Switch “Petal Notes” and “Note pad in the dock” on or off, pick “Paper or Liquid Glass” under “Look”, and set “New notes go to” and “Kept in” under “Notebooks”. See Petal Notes.

Petal AI

Manage your AI connection and behavior for Petal AI and Your own agents:

  • “Your AI” (a row per AI), “Paying” and “Usage”.
  • “Chat”: “If you write while it works” (“Steers it” or “Waits its turn”), “Chat with” (when both AIs are on), “Model”, “Effort” and “Access” (“Ask before risky steps” — shown as “Safe steps only” with Codex — or “Full access”).
  • “Using your apps”: “Let Petal AI use your apps”, ““Ask Petal AI” by selected text” and “Let Petal AI work on a hidden screen”.
  • “In the background”: “Quick answers” and “Usage ring around the AI button”.
  • “What Petal AI should always know” and “Other AI agents” (“Let other AI agents change Petal”).

Vault

“Search” your saved items, choose “New item”, classify older unsorted items under “Tell Petal what each one is” when present, use “Import and export” (“Import” and “Export”), and manage “Other AI agents” grants. See The vault.

Routines

Turn all routines on or off with “Run my routines”, start one under “Make a routine” (“Ask Petal AI”), manage “Your routines” once you have any, and review “What they did”. See Routines.

Privacy

Control what stays off-limits and what Petal keeps:

  • “Kept private” (“Add an app”) and “What Petal AI did”.
  • “Memory”: “Remember what helps” and “What it remembers”.
  • “Allowed without asking” (shown once you have saved Always approvals).
  • “What macOS lets Petal see”: “Permissions”.

About

Under “Petal”, check your version and links to “Website” and “GitHub”. “Help” holds “Check Petal” (“Check again”, “Fix these” and “Report a problem…”). “Updates” holds “Update automatically” and “Check for updates” (“Check now”). See Support.

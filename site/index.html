Before you install

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. The dock and your notes work without one.

What leaves my Mac?

What you ask the AI, through your own Claude Code or Codex.

Why can't macOS check Petal?

It has no Apple Developer ID. Open it once, then System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. The Terminal line skips this.

What does it ask permission for?

Accessibility, to see windows and use apps. Screen Recording, for window previews.

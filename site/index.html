The dock that knows
what you're doing.

Petal gives every workspace a focus mode, holds back what can wait, and has Claude built in. Want it different? Just ask.

Download for Linux macOS soon Windows soon
curl -fsSL https://petal-dock.vercel.app/install.sh | bash
For Omarchy on Linux. Installs in seconds, updates itself.

A dock with a sense of focus

Modes like Building, Writing and Social, each with their own apps and rules. Every workspace remembers its mode, so swiping between workspaces swaps the dock too.

🌸

Focus modes

Each mode has its own apps and color. Apps from other modes stay out of the way.

🔕

Held notifications

Messages from outside your mode wait quietly. The ones that can't wait still get through.

🧭

Gentle nudges

Drifting to a Social tab while Building? A small card asks if you meant to switch.

✨

Everything a dock should do

Badges, progress rings, previews, now playing, folder stacks, drop to open, minimize into the dock.

Claude, right in your dock

A real Claude Code session a click away. It can change your dock, your setup or your files, and asks before anything risky. A ring around its button shows how much of your Claude limit you've used.

“Make the dock background black.” “Stop the icons from bouncing.” “Add a Gaming mode with Steam and Discord.” “Why is my laptop slow right now?”

Uses your own Claude Code login. Optional: everything else works without it.

Build from source

Petal is open source (MIT).

git clone https://github.com/edgetr/petal && cd petal && ./install.sh && omarchy restart shell
